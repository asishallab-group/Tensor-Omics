Module for quantifying how much one trajectory (a "factor") contributes to another (a "dependent") over time.

Contributions are computed per timepoint as the product of both series' deviations from a chosen baseline, for raw expression trajectories as well as for their velocity (first difference) and acceleration (second difference) derivatives. Statistical significance of an observed contribution can be assessed via a permutation test that recomputes the same contribution against a randomly chosen other sample.


Uses

    • f42_safeguard
    • tox_conversions
    • iso_c_binding
    • tox_errors
