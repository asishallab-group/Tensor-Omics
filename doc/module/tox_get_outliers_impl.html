compute_family_scaling_impl compute_rdi_impl identify_outliers_impl detect_outliers_impl

Gene outliers, from how far each gene sits from its family's centroid.

The pipeline is three steps, each callable on its own. compute_rdi turns raw distances into a relative distance index, scaled per family so families of different spread are comparable. compute_family_scaling fits that scaling with LOESS against family size. identify_outliers applies the threshold and reports which genes exceed it.

detect_outliers runs all three in one call, and is the entry point to reach for first.
